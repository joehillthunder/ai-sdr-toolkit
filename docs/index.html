Rollout briefing — for the person standing this up

Give the team a queue worth working, not a chatbot to babysit.

ai-sdr-toolkit turns hiring surges, funding events, tech adoption, and website language into a scored, prioritized account queue — then only spends agent time and rep attention on the accounts that clear the bar. This is the plan for putting it in front of an actual SDR team.

Audience: Head of Sales / SDR manager Time to first pilot: ~1 week Repo: github.com/joehillthunder/ai-sdr-toolkit

00 — Before the team sees it

Point it at your real ICP, not the demo data

The repo ships with a bundled offline dataset so anyone can run it in five minutes. Before a rep touches it, replace that with your actual targeting — copy examples/icp.yaml, then edit the industries, headcount range, and the job-title / tech / homepage keywords that actually correlate with a good-fit account for your product.

01 — The mechanism worth explaining first

Score gates spend. That's the whole idea.

Every account gets scored — that part is deterministic and free. Only accounts that clear nurture_threshold get a research + qualification call; only accounts the qualification agent verdicts qualified get a personalized, signal-referencing sequence drafted. This is the actual mechanism behind "more output per rep" — attention and LLM spend go where they're likely to pay off, not evenly across every company you can find.

Teach this first

Every dollar of model spend and every minute of rep attention in this system is already means-tested by the score before a rep ever sees it. That's the pitch for the team: the queue is pre-filtered, not pre-written.

02 — Sequence the rollout, don't big-bang it

One rep, then the team, then a standing tuning cadence

A pilot catches a badly-tuned ICP before it wastes the whole team's week. Don't skip it.

PH.01

Pilot — one rep, one week

Your most technically curious SDR runs the pipeline daily and works the queue top-down instead of their usual list-building. Compare against their own recent baseline: accounts researched/day, sequences sent/day, reply rate.

PH.02

Team rollout — weeks two and three

Run the training session in §03. Every rep gets the pipeline, the production ICP file, and clear expectations on what to trust versus what to personalize (§04).

PH.03

Scale & tune — week four onward

Weekly review of funnel metrics per rep (§05). The ICP file keeps changing as you learn what actually books meetings — this phase never really ends.

04 — What a rep's day looks like

Daily loop, with a weekly loop wrapped around it

The pipeline drafts; the rep decides. That line doesn't move, no matter how good the model gets.

Non-negotiable

AI output is a draft, not a send button. The qualification verdict is driven by the deterministic score, but the dossier and sequence copy come from a model — treat them like a first draft from a very fast, very literal junior AE. A rep who sends a drafted sequence unread is doing it wrong.

06 — FAQ

Questions that come up in week one

The dossier said something wrong about a company.

Expected occasionally — it's a model summarizing incomplete signal data, not a verified research report. That's exactly why personalization is a human step, not an autosend.

A great-fit account showed up as below_threshold.

Check whether signal_keywords actually cover how that account's job posts or homepage copy is worded, and add the missing terms to icp/production.yaml.

Do we need --live every day?

Offline mode costs nothing and scores identically — fine for testing ICP changes. Use --live for the sequences reps will actually send; offline dossiers/sequences are template placeholders, not real copy.

Can reps use their own ICP tweaks?

Keep one production ICP file the manager owns; let reps propose changes rather than everyone forking their own — otherwise the queue stops being comparable rep-to-rep.
